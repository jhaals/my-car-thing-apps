# Apple Calendar

## 0.1.1

Fix Calendar access in the desktop app with a separately authorized Mac helper app.

## 0.1.0

- Show time until a meeting in hours and minutes for waits of an hour or more.
- Display the clock and meeting times in 24-hour format.
- Show today’s ongoing and upcoming Apple Calendar events, including recurring and all-day meetings.
- Show Google Meet links from event descriptions, URL fields and locations in meeting details.
- Press the wheel in meeting details to open Google Meet in the Mac’s browser.
- Browse with the wheel or touch, open meeting details, and refresh with the Back button below the wheel.
- Read calendars through a local macOS EventKit helper and desktop extension.
